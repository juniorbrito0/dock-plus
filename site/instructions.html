Get started

Up and running
in two minutes

Install Dock+, arrange your widgets and grant a few macOS permissions. Here's the whole thing, including why each permission is needed.

Install

  1. Download and move to Applications

    Download Dock+, open the disk image and drag Dock+.app into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it

    Double-click Dock+ in Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings. Dock+ lives in the menu bar: it has no Dock icon of its own and doesn't appear when you press Command + Tab.

  3. Open Settings

    Click the Dock+ icon in the menu bar (four small squares) and choose Settings & Permissions…. Grant the permissions for the widgets you want, and turn on Start Dock+ automatically at login if you like.

Settings

General

  • Permissions: see what's granted and grant what's missing in one place.
  • Dock position: bottom or top of the screen.
  • Keep windows from covering Dock+ (on by default): windows open, move and resize normally but stop at the edge of Dock+. Full-screen windows and dialogs are left alone, and minimizing Dock+ gives the space back.
  • Accent color: eight presets or any custom color. The whole dock changes instantly.
  • Startup: start Dock+ automatically at login.

Widgets

  • Add a widget with the plus button, remove it with the minus button.
  • Reorder by dragging the ≡ handle or with the up and down arrows. The top of the list is the left end of the dock.

Content

  • Manage your favorite apps, bookmarks, folders and quick actions. Drag the ≡ handle to reorder.

Need the screen back? Click any empty spot on the bar to shrink Dock+ to a small handle; click the handle to restore it. If your system Dock auto-hides, Dock+ lifts out of its way when it appears.

How to

  • Open your next meeting: click the Calendar tile.
  • Open an email: click the Mail tile, then click the email.
  • Copy a verification code: open 1Password, search, and click the clock button on the result.
  • Search the web: type in Chrome Search and press Return. Click the list button to jump to an open tab.
  • Save a bookmark: drag a link from your browser onto the Bookmarks tile.
  • Show one reminders list: open the Reminders tile and pick the list from its menu.

Permissions, and why each is needed

Dock+ only asks for what your widgets use. You can review or remove any permission later in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security.

📅

Calendar

So the Calendar widget can show your next event and open it in Calendar. Events are read on your Mac and never leave it.

✅

Reminders

So the Reminders widget can show, add, complete and reschedule your reminders, locally.

📍

Location

So the Clock & Weather widget can show conditions where you are. Only your approximate location is used, and only for the weather.

🤖

Automation

So Dock+ can talk to other apps on your Mac: Music or Spotify (playback), Mail (open, archive, delete), System Events (Mail's snooze), Google Chrome (list and switch tabs) and Dia (list and switch tabs). macOS asks once per app, the first time it's needed.

♿

Accessibility

So the Emoji widget can paste into the app you're typing in, and so Dock+ can keep windows from covering it by gently resizing windows that overlap the bar.

🔑

1Password CLI (Touch ID)

The 1Password widget uses 1Password's own command-line tool, op, which you approve with Touch ID. Copied items clear from your clipboard after 90 seconds and are never logged or sent anywhere.

Skipping a permission only switches off the feature that needs it. Everything else keeps working.

Setting up 1Password

  1. Install the 1Password CLI

    Run brew install 1password-cli, or follow 1Password's guide.

  2. Turn on the integration

    In the 1Password app, open Settings ▸ Developer and turn on Integrate with 1Password CLI.

  3. Approve Dock+

    Open the 1Password widget (click Retry if needed) and approve the prompt with Touch ID.
