The catalog

Sixteen widgets,
all on one bar

Mix and match. Each widget is one tile that does one thing well, and most let you act, not just look. Tags show what stays on-device and the one widget that makes a network call.

🕘

Clock & Weather Network

Time, date, temperature, conditions and your city in one tile. Weather comes from the free Open-Meteo service, using your approximate location. No account, no key.

🎧

Music Local

Now playing with play/pause and skip. Click for your current playlist. Works with Apple Music and Spotify (the playlist view is Apple Music only).

📅

Calendar Local

Your next event, with long titles wrapping onto two lines. Shown in your accent color, then amber under 30 minutes and pulsing red under 10. Click to open the event in Calendar.

✅

Reminders Local

How many reminders are open. Click to add a reminder, tick one off, or move it to Today, Tomorrow or Next Week. Show all lists or just one.

✉️

Mail Local

Your latest inbox messages. Click one to open that exact email in Mail. Hover to snooze it (Mail's Remind Me), archive it or delete it.

📊

System Local

Live CPU and memory use, read directly from macOS.

🔋

Battery Local

Charge level and whether you're plugged in.

🚀

Apps Local

Your favorite apps on the bar. One click to open.

📁

Folders Local

Pinned folders. Click for a preview of what's inside, double-click to open in Finder.

🔖

Bookmarks Local

Drag a link onto the tile to save it, then click to open it later.

🧭

Dia Tabs Local

Your open tabs in the Dia browser. Click one to jump straight to it.

😀

Emoji Local

Your five most-used emoji plus the full picker. Click one and it's pasted into the app you're typing in.

✦

Ask Claude Local

Type a question and press Return to start a new chat in the Claude desktop app.

🔑

1Password Local

Search your logins. Every result has buttons to copy the username, the password or the verification code, or to open the website. The clipboard clears itself after 90 seconds.

⚙️

Quick Actions Local

One-click shortcuts: Screenshot, Screenshot to Clipboard, Sleep Display, Lock Screen, Screen Saver, Empty Trash, Toggle Dark Mode, Mute/Unmute and Open Downloads. Pick the ones you want; the tile grows to fit them.

🔎

Chrome Search Local

Type a search or a web address and press Return to open it in a new Google Chrome tab. The list button shows your open Chrome tabs; click one to switch to it.

Pick your set.

Turn on only what you need, drag the tiles into your order, and choose your accent color in Settings.
