The idea

Your Dock holds apps. Dock+ holds information.

The macOS Dock launches things. Dock+ adds a second strip of live tiles right above it: the things you keep switching apps to check. It shows on every Desktop, never steals focus, and lifts out of the way when an auto-hidden Dock appears.

🪟

Windows stay clear

Like the real Dock, windows stop at the top edge of Dock+ instead of sliding underneath. Move and resize them as usual. You can turn this off in Settings.

⚡

Live and clickable

Weather, music, CPU, battery, your next meeting and your reminders refresh on their own, and most tiles do something when you click them.

🔒

On your Mac

No account, no analytics, no tracking. The only thing Dock+ sends anywhere is your approximate location, for the weather.

🎛️

Yours to arrange

Turn any of the 16 widgets on or off, drag them into your order, put the bar at the top or bottom, and pick your own accent color.

🤏

Out of the way

Click an empty spot on the bar to shrink it to a small handle. One click brings it back.

🍎

Native and light

A small SwiftUI app that lives in the menu bar. It can start with your Mac and remembers your layout.

Widgets

Sixteen widgets. One glance.

Each tile does one useful thing well. Control music, open an email, paste an emoji or copy a password without leaving what you're doing.

📅

Calendar

Your next meeting, colored by how soon it starts. Click to open it in Calendar.

✉️

Mail

Your latest emails. Click one to open it, or snooze, archive and delete from the tile.

🔑

🔎

🎧

🌤️
